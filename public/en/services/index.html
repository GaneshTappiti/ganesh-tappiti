0304Method

One method, five steps

A brand, a product or a campus launch: the path is the same, and you always know which step things are at.

  1. 01

    Step 1: Discover

    I start with the problem, not the deliverable.

    A call and a look at what already exists, plus the questions nobody asked yet: who is this for, what happens today, and what would make it worth building.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Define

    A written scope you can hold me to.

    What is in, what is out, what it costs and in what order. Small enough to ship, clear enough that you can say no to the extras.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Design

    Interfaces and systems, designed in the open.

    Wireframes to hi-fi in Figma, reviewed with you at each step. Components and rules documented as I build, so nothing depends on me being around.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Build

    Real code, shipped to real users.

    React, TypeScript, Supabase and Tailwind. Loading states, empty states, errors and keyboard access are part of the build, not a later phase.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Ship & learn

    Launch, watch, adjust.

    Analytics and feedback after release, then the fixes that matter. I stay reachable after handover, and the docs are yours.

0405FAQ

Got questions?

Straight answers about how I work. Anything else, just email — I reply within a day.

Email me
What can I hire you for?

Four things, often together: strategy & consulting, brand & creative, web & digital, and campaigns & media. Most projects start in one and end up using two or three, because the strategy, the interface and the build share the same decisions.

Do you work alone or with a team?

Solo by default, which is why there are no handovers: the person who scopes the work designs it and builds it. For bigger launches I bring in a trusted group — a photographer, a copywriter, another developer — and you get one point of contact and one invoice.

How long does a project take?

A brand system is usually two to four weeks. A product MVP, four to eight. A campus campaign runs across a semester, with the launch concentrated in the first three weeks. You get a written timeline before anything starts, and a weekly note while it runs.

How do we start, and what does it cost?

Send one paragraph about the problem to ganeshtappitii@gmail.com or use the contact form. I reply within a day with either a call slot or an honest “this isn’t my thing”. Pricing is a fixed quote per scope, agreed in writing before work starts.
