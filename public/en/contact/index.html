04FAQ

Got questions?

The questions that come up before a first call. Anything else, just email — I answer every message.

Email me
How fast do you reply?

Within one working day, usually sooner. If you write on a Friday evening IST, expect Monday morning. Every message comes to me directly — there is no inbox team.

What should I put in the form?

A paragraph is enough: what you are building, who it is for, and when you need it. Links to a deck, a Figma file or a half-finished site help more than a polished brief.

Do you take on small projects?

Yes. A one-page site, a logo fix, a two-week prototype — if the problem is interesting and the deadline is real, size is not the issue. I would rather do a small thing well than a large thing badly.

What does it cost?

A fixed quote per scope, agreed in writing before work starts. Small brand or web projects typically start around ₹40,000; product builds are scoped after a discovery call. Student and campus projects are priced differently — ask.

Can we work across time zones?

Yes. I am in IST (GMT+5:30), which overlaps with Europe in the morning and with the US east coast late in my day. Async-first: written updates, recorded walkthroughs, and one standing call a week.

Are you available for a full-time role?

I am finishing my B.Tech and running Clstr, so I take on freelance and contract work rather than full-time roles. Internships and collaborations around product, design or campus programmes are always worth a conversation.
