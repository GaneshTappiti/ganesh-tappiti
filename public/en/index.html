07FAQ

Got questions?

The questions people ask first.

Contact me
What do you actually do?

I design and build digital products end to end: product strategy, interface and design systems, then the front end and back end that make it real. My work sits across four disciplines: strategy and consulting, brand and creative, web and digital, campaigns and media.

Are you a designer or a developer?

Both, and that is the point. I move between Figma and a code editor in the same afternoon, which means fewer handoffs, faster decisions, and interfaces that survive contact with real data.

Do you work with startups or students?

Mostly early-stage teams and campus communities. I have founded one product myself (Clstr), so I know what it is like to have a deadline, a small budget and no room for a redesign.

Can you work with a team outside India?

Yes. I am based in Visakhapatnam (IST) and keep overlap hours with Singapore, London and the US. Async-first, with a short call per week if that helps.

What does an engagement look like?

A short call to understand the problem, a written scope with a fixed price, then weekly increments you can click through. Nothing gets built for three weeks and revealed at the end.

Do you take on brand and campaign work?

Yes. Identity, design systems, pitch decks, campus launches and community programmes. The E-Cell REC brand system and Clstr’s campus growth both came from this mix.
