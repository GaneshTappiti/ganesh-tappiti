Got questions?

What kind of work is on this page?

Products I have designed and built, brands I have shaped, and campus programmes I have run. Most of it is early-stage: a problem, a small team, a real deadline.

Can I see the live products?

Clstr is live at clstr.in (opens in a new tab), Spark at spark.clstr.in (opens in a new tab), and the Vizag community at vizag.clstr.in (opens in a new tab). The other projects are in various states of shipping; email me and I will walk you through the ones that are relevant to you.

Did you work on these alone or in a team?

Clstr is a small team where I lead product and design and build the front end. The campus and brand projects were collaborations with student volunteers, designers and developers — credited where it matters.

Can you build something similar for us?

Yes, with a caveat: no project is copied. We start from your problem, your users and your budget, then reuse what genuinely applies. Tell me about it.
