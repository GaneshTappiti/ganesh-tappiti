About

Product designer and full-stack engineer. Founder of Clstr. Based in Visakhapatnam, India, building for campuses everywhere.

The path here

I started with design, stayed for the code, and never picked one over the other.

I grew up in Visakhapatnam, studied computer science at Raghu Engineering College, and spent most of my free time redrawing interfaces I thought could be better. Then I learned to build them.

Clstr started as a question from a friend: why is there no place where students from different campuses can actually find each other? It became a product across 33+ campuses. Somewhere between the brand work for E-Cell REC and the late-night deploys, the two halves of the job became one.

Three things guide the work

  • Curiosity

    I read changelogs for fun. The fastest way to make something better is to notice how other people solved it, then take the part that actually applies.

  • Craft

    Details are not decoration. Spacing, loading states, empty states and error messages are where trust is won or lost, so they get the same attention as the hero.

  • Usefulness

    A beautiful screen nobody needs is a failure. Every project starts with the question: what changes for the person using this?
